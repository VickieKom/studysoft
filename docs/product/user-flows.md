# User flows

The journeys the platform exists for. Each step names the section that does the work; the details are in [Modules](modules.md).

## 1. A new student

1. The teacher creates a student card. Its status is "Обсуждение".
2. The teacher holds an introductory meeting with the parent and the child: a level check, an explanation of the method, getting to know each other. It is free. An optional trial lesson of 30–60 minutes is free too.
3. On the card the teacher fills in the CEFR level, interests, the results of the level check, the textbook and the gaps to work on.
4. Later: the child picks a mascot from a few options (stage 3).
5. The status becomes "Занимается". The student and parent links are ready to send.

## 2. Preparing a lesson

1. In the schedule the teacher sees the next lesson and opens it.
2. The platform has already pulled the materials of the student's current unit: the cut-out pages of the student's book and the workbook, the unit's audio tracks, the whole textbook.
3. The assistant proposes a draft lesson plan from the unit, the student's gaps and interests (stage 2). The teacher edits it; a manual plan is stage 1.
4. The teacher adds a warm-up, games, videos and phrases from the library.
5. About 15 minutes before the lesson the teacher looks at the homework results.

## 3. Running a lesson

1. The video call runs in an external video-call service.
2. "Начать урок" opens **two windows, like a presentation in presenter mode**:
   - **the stage** ("Экран для ученика") — content and a board only; this window is shared in the call;
   - **the control panel** ("Пульт урока") — the plan, notes, homework mistakes, switching materials; the student never sees it.
3. On one laptop screen the windows stand side by side (stage about two thirds on the left, panel on the right); with a second monitor the panel goes there.
4. The teacher switches materials on the panel — a textbook page, the workbook, a track, a game, homework mistakes — and the stage changes at once.
5. The teacher writes on the board in the stage window; words marked there go to a "to vocabulary" list on the panel.
6. A game either opens on the stage and the teacher drives it, or is sent to the student as a link.

## 4. After the lesson, in a couple of clicks

One form, "Завершить урок":

1. Mark the lesson as held: it goes to the lesson history and is charged to the payment balance.
2. Write a short note: what was covered, what to work on.
3. Move the marked board words and the unit's vocabulary to the student's vocabulary.
4. Tick "Юнит пройден" to move the student to the next unit.
5. Publish the new homework: it appears behind the student's permanent link, due by the next lesson.
6. Optionally save a snapshot of the board to the lesson history (stage 2).

## 5. The student does homework

1. The student opens their permanent link on a phone and sees the new homework.
2. They do it in 10–15 minutes, one task per screen, in a playful format with large buttons. A mascot comes in stage 3.
3. Answers are saved as they go; closing the page loses nothing.
4. One attempt: after "Сдать" it cannot be changed. Only the teacher can reopen a submitted homework (pending a decision in #36).
5. The final screen praises the student and shows "X верно, Y неверно" without saying which answers were wrong: mistakes are reviewed in the lesson.
6. The results go to the teacher's "Проверка домашек".

## 6. The teacher checks homework

1. "Проверка домашек" lists submitted and missing homework.
2. Tasks with one right answer are already checked against the key.
3. The teacher checks open answers, texts, photos and voice recordings, marks them right or wrong, and can override an automatic result.
4. The results appear in the lesson mode, on the "Домашка" stage.

## 7. Monthly assessment and report (stage 2)

1. Once a month the assistant builds a test from the units covered since the last one. The teacher edits it.
2. The test takes about half a lesson, behind the student link.
3. Reading, listening, grammar and vocabulary are checked automatically; writing and speaking by the teacher.
4. The result per skill, in percent, goes to the dashboard.
5. The assistant drafts a report to the parent from the teacher's template, the data and the notes. The teacher edits it and clicks "Отправить".
6. The parent receives an email with a PDF and sees the report and the dashboard behind their link.
